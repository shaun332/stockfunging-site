What is Stock Finance?

Your inventory is an asset — make it work for you

Stock funding is a revolving credit facility secured against the value of your business's inventory. As you replenish stock, your facility resets — giving you continuous access to working capital without straining your cash flow.

How a Revolving Facility Works

1

Stock is Assessed

Lender values your eligible inventory — finished goods, raw materials, or traded stock.

2

Facility is Agreed

You receive a credit line typically 50–70% of stock value, available to draw at any time.

3

Draw as Needed

Access funds to pay suppliers, manage seasonal peaks, or grow your product range.

4

Repay as Stock Sells

Repayments flex with your sales cycle. As stock moves, your facility refreshes automatically.

Is stock funding right for your business?

  • You hold physical stock or inventory on your balance sheet
  • You need to buy stock before you can sell it (advance supplier payments)
  • Your business has seasonal stock requirements or unpredictable demand
  • You want to take advantage of bulk buying discounts from suppliers
  • You're growing and your current working capital can't keep pace
  • You want a flexible facility that grows alongside your stock levels

Why Stock Funding?

The smarter way to manage working capital

Unlike a fixed-term business loan, a revolving stock facility is designed to flex around your trading patterns — giving you more control and less financial strain.

Revolving — Not Fixed

Draw, repay, and redraw as many times as you need. Your facility resets as stock sells, unlike a one-time loan that depletes and disappears.

Preserve Your Cash Flow

Stop using your operating cash to buy stock. Free up liquidity to pay staff, cover overheads, and invest in growth simultaneously.

Scale With Your Business

As your stock value grows, your facility can grow with it. No need to renegotiate from scratch as your business expands.

Better Supplier Terms

Pay suppliers promptly and in full, unlocking early payment discounts and securing preferential pricing on bulk orders.

No Hidden Surprises

AIM presents every offer with full fee transparency. You'll always know exactly what you're paying before you commit to anything.
